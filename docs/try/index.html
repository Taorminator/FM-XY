Make music

Choose CHORD and set ROOT and TYPE with the first two encoders. Every key triggers a full chord of the selected TYPE, transposed from ROOT. Highlighted keys only show the suggested next chord. PLAY starts the demo loop and REC enables overdub. Filter movements on the FX screen are recorded during REC.

Step-Sequencer

SEQ opens the 16-step grid. White keys add or remove steps on the selected track. On DRUM, encoder 2 chooses the drum lane. Use GLO to mix your tracks.
